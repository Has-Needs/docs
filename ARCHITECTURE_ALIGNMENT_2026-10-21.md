# Organization documentation alignment — 21 October 2026

This update carries the latest architecture discussion across the five Has-Needs repositories: docs, Home, .github, code, and live.

## Clarifications carried through

- A Need is a smart contract with standard templates and custom options; the WORKING state connects all involved Has and Needs while it executes.
- Delegation, open-ended Community participation, governance terms, and composable API streams use the existing mechanism.
- Community storage preserves permissions; sender/driver handoffs and pooled versus individual receiving preserve authority.
- The fulfilling worker informs the local ontology's self-pruning tree, with hierarchy retained for fallback.
- Identical receipts are written simultaneously, corroborated with encrypted contents, and followed by independent outward verification hops.
- Agreed no-deal and expiry outcomes are recorded and can affect personal filters without turning unaccepted candidates into negative history.
- Civic contribution and expenditure evidence supports reconciliation and bottleneck analysis. Civic encryption/access versus mandatory public-funding transparency remains an open policy choice.
