# Has-Needs

**What do you have? What do you need? What can we accomplish together?**

**Start here:** [One-page introduction](INTRODUCTION.md) · [Research thesis for academics and investors](RESEARCH_WHITE_PAPER_COMPANION.md) · [Paradigm Delta](Has-Needs_Paradigm_Delta_Review_2026-10-14.md) · [V1 technical specification](Has-Needs-Spec-v1.md)

**At a glance:** Has-Needs uses sovereign HAS, NEED, and WORKING relationships, voluntary commitments, and reciprocal evidence to enable deliberately emergent coordination across disaster response, civic life, agriculture, research, and commerce. A farmer's old phone can passively observe field conditions, contribute to local predictions, and support voluntary compensated knowledge exchange with governance and researchers. This is a **post-capitalist, non-extractive economic design**: participants retain authority over their contributions and the terms of exchange. The proposed Globe UI is a literacy- and culturally agnostic medium of relevant interaction, supported by equivalent accessible modalities.

### A foundation for sovereign participation

Every person has inherent dignity and agency. Society is sustained by exchanges of value: care, knowledge, time, labor, resources, and commitments. Yet the institutions and platforms that facilitate these exchanges often control how our identities, relationships, and contributions are recognized and recorded. We participate in society while surrendering control over the evidence of our participation.

**Has-Needs is designed as the first comprehensive societal tool set for sovereign human exchange.** It aims to make that control practical and enforceable for individuals and the communities they form.

The idea is remarkably simple. People express what they have and what they need. They discover opportunities to cooperate, make voluntary commitments, choose what to share, and preserve their own records of completed exchanges. The same foundation supports mutual aid, education, commerce, governance, research, and everyday life.

Consider a person trapped beneath earthquake rubble. They know their condition and may sense things even responders equipped with drones cannot independently discover. With Has-Needs, they can express a Need for rescue and share firsthand knowledge through participating local-first mesh devices within range. The opportunity to act and potentially be heard begins before assistance arrives.

**The network begins in each person's immediate sphere of awareness and capability.** Nearby people and devices cooperate; relationships and mobility expand what becomes possible. Wider networks and institutions can extend that reach, but are not prerequisites for beginning.

**Help should follow people's needs, not delivery schedules.** Aid often arrives in batches; people's needs arise at different times. Has-Needs lets each Need remain discoverable and progress independently as resources become available. The same mechanism serves everyday life, community cooperation, and participatory research—without requiring people to fit an institution's timetable.

**Portable evidence belongs with the person.** Has-Needs lets people authorize specific uses of their information and carry independently verifiable evidence of participation across communities and jurisdictions. Verification does not depend on a sophisticated local institution; the same personal evidence can support everyday exchange, commerce, and migration.

**Human exchange comes first. Institutions participate without taking ownership of the people or their relationships.**

Has-Needs is designed around human nature, working backward from desirable outcomes such as agency, dignity, reciprocity, trust, accountability, and resilience. Simple interactions allow increasingly sophisticated cooperation to emerge naturally. People and communities retain authority over their identities, meanings, knowledge, and participation.

Its architecture emerged from decades of work in geographic information, community self-determination, and emergency response, crystallizing during the 2010 Haiti earthquake response and undergoing more than fifteen years of deliberate development.

**Cooperation should begin with people able to exercise authority over their participation.**

**Reciprocal value begins with the individual.** In a disaster, a trapped person may hold information rescuers urgently need—their location and condition—while rescuers hold the capability to help. The same principle supports a newcomer discovering local resources and offering everyday abilities. Has-Needs makes these opportunities visible and voluntary, without making humanitarian aid conditional on exchange.

**Sovereign commerce.** Businesses can publish standing Needs while individuals' Persona Managers privately evaluate relevance; only voluntary responses reach the business. Creators, tradespeople, and service providers can selectively offer attested capabilities and professional histories—including apprenticeship provenance, project patterns, and reciprocal receipts—without surrendering their identity, contacts, or unrelated records. The same open-ended Hases and Needs support tangible and intangible value, voluntary exchange, and locally governed markets.
