# Has-Needs
## A social protocol that begins with the individual

*What do you have? What do you need? What can we accomplish together?*

Our lives are made of relationships: the things we need, what we can contribute, the people we trust, and what we accomplish together. Yet most digital systems place an organization or platform at the center. We enter their databases, follow their rules, and leave our history behind when we go elsewhere.

**Has-Needs reverses that arrangement.** It is a protocol designed around the individual, with a protected, portable record of participation that does not depend on any one network, platform, or institution. You can carry evidence of your capabilities, experience, commitments, and accomplishments into new situations, while choosing which parts others may see. Community attestations and records of completed exchanges can support what you say about yourself, without requiring you to reveal your entire identity or history.

### From you, out into society—and back again

You express a **Need**—something you hope to accomplish—or a **Has**—something you can contribute. Matching tools, including AI, help find relevant possibilities, beginning in communities and places where the exchange could work. **They suggest; you choose.** No proposed match becomes an agreement just because software recommends it.

When the participants accept the terms, their exchange enters **Working**. An associated pending record tracks the commitment and its authorized progress. Only when the outcome is resolved do the participants receive matching receipts in their own personal histories. Unfinished work is not misrepresented as success.

The flow is important: your request goes outward; replies and offers come back to you; your choices determine what proceeds; and the resulting evidence returns to inform **your** continuing record. The network helps the interaction happen. It does not become the owner of your identity, relationships, or achievements.

### Consider a flooded community

A road is blocked. Someone needs it cleared so food and medical supplies can pass. An operator offers an excavator; a neighbor offers knowledge of a safe route. Has-Needs can suggest the connection, but the people involved decide whether to act together.

Once they agree, others who have permission can see that help has been committed without confusing it with a cleared road. If the work stalls, the outstanding commitment remains visible to authorized participants. When the job is resolved, matching receipts preserve evidence of the outcome. The people involved can later show relevant experience or accomplishments without surrendering unrelated personal information.

Residents are not merely reporting data into an agency's system. They remain participants who can ask, choose, contribute, receive responses, and retain the results.

### Designed for ordinary life and extraordinary circumstances

Has-Needs was conceived to **mitigate trauma** by preserving people's voice and ability to act during disasters—and to **reduce friction in human exchanges throughout society**. A neighbor borrowing a tool, farmers organizing transport, researchers inviting participants, a municipality responding to residents, and a community recovering from an earthquake all use the same basic pattern.

One simple interaction can support resource discovery, coordinated commitments, and reliable records of results. As successful exchanges accumulate, communities can develop practical knowledge about what has worked, while individuals retain control over what they disclose. Groups can form and pool efforts by consent rather than by submitting themselves to a social platform.

Has-Needs is designed to adapt across connected services, local networks, and low-bandwidth communication. The goal is continuity: the relationships and capacities people build in everyday life remain available when circumstances become difficult.

**Has-Needs puts the individual at the center of the social system: able to reach outward, act with others, and bring the knowledge of each interaction home.**
