# Has-Needs: Sovereign Knowledge and Post-Capitalist Exchange
*Research and interpretive companion, 20 October 2026. Founder-proposed mechanisms and hypotheses, not V1 normative requirements.*

## Passive contribution, active sovereignty
A farmer can place an old phone on a stick to observe field conditions. With suitable power, sensors and connectivity, observations can support locally relevant predictions while requiring little ongoing attention. Farmers can voluntarily pool permitted observations and negotiate compensated data-use or research agreements with governance bodies and international researchers. Consent, access limits, compensation and downstream use remain governed by explicit participant authority; collecting observations does not automatically authorize resale or disclosure.

## Economic inversion
Rather than treating rural communities as raw data sources for institutional extraction, Has-Needs proposes that individuals and communities become sovereign producers of useful knowledge and negotiate the terms of exchange. This is a post-capitalist, non-extractive design orientation: it shifts control of information and economic relationships toward participants without requiring the abolition of markets or monetary compensation. Whether specific arrangements deliver equitable returns is a research question.

## Globe UI, locality, and efficacy
The Globe UI is intended as a literacy- and culturally agnostic medium of exchange and relevant interaction, not just a geographic display. Pictorial surroundings can connect information to a person's mental representation of place, reducing dependence on written words and imposed categories. Predictive observations can inform preventive choices and opportunities. Both active decisions and passively sustained, authorized contributions may support perceived efficacy. Those psychological effects are intentional hypotheses, not measured outcomes. Equivalent nonvisual, speech, text, SMS and assistive pathways are essential; geographic relevance does not require publishing precise whereabouts.

## Sovereign Survivor +1
The founder's architectural progression begins with making human inputs valid and actionable without surrendering PII, restoring agency early in trauma, and informing the sovereign endpoint first. Since disasters may disrupt any part of a hierarchy, each surviving participant should be able to coordinate without depending on intact centralized command. Emerging local relationships provide scaffolding into which institutions can reconnect.

## Design provenance and personal history
The founder reports formative experiences of repeated childhood moves and foster care, loss of personal keepsakes, early exposure to Buckminster Fuller, peer counseling beginning in 1985, emergency response and specialist debriefing, and participation in a UCSF stress/trauma study. In seventh-grade English class, a teacher offered stones collected from Ishi's home region while students read his story; the founder, recently displaced and sustained by nature, formed a lasting personal connection. The original dedication to Ishi should be recovered rather than invented.

Early Nomads International and international BBS/2600 participation revealed the collapse of distance, while commercial enclosure made locality and direct relationships more important. The founder reports that more than fifty moves before age twenty sharpened the question: who does a person's location matter to, when, and how can relevant interests intersect? Map Kibera, WaveFax, crisis mapping, Uchaguzi, UN-HHI exercises, participatory research, and work at Amazon and IBM further informed the architecture. These are founder recollections and interpretive provenance, not independently verified historical findings.

The founder's hypothesis is that a sovereign, self-quantified individual can flourish whether rooted or mobile: continuity of history, agency and trustable relationships should not depend on continuity of address. Graeber and Wengrow's *The Dawn of Everything* is a later intellectual influence, not proof of universal semi-migratory behavior or therapeutic benefit.
