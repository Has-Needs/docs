# A pooled delivery and a request that finds its worker

A Community forms around a Need for a collective space to coordinate repairs under its chosen DAO policies. Purpose, governance, responsible parties, and permissions are terms of a standard smart-contract template with custom options. Its one-to-many match can remain open-ended in the WORKING state.

The Community pools a Need for materials and authorizes a representative to receive them. At the warehouse, the driver scans and accepts package A; the handing-over entity records its side of the handoff. The agreed truck departure time does not wait for every expected package. The journal records what is actually aboard.

At the destination, the Community representative scans the pooled items under delegated authority. Another parcel was negotiated individually: its recipient must appear or explicitly delegate receiving permission. The driver cannot infer that authority from carrying the parcel.

A municipal repair request follows authorized delegation through the org chart until a worker resolves it. That worker's fulfillment is the significant outcome for the local ontology. The next relevant request can take the most recent effective route; if the worker is absent, the hierarchy remains available for alternatives. This is the self-pruning tree.

When the Community's originator cannot comfortably store the growing communications, its Need authorizes responsible parties to stream them to a secure storage channel through the composable API. The same API mechanism supplies personal data streams. Storage preserves the permission structure and does not entitle the provider to read or act beyond the contract.

At each authorized resolution, the complete exchange—including delegation, delivery handoffs, and communications—is encapsulated in identical receipts written simultaneously to required participants' chains and mutually verified. Encrypted entries remain corroborable. An agreed expiry or no-deal resolution is recorded through that same mechanism.

The contribution and expenditure record helps reconcile the budget and identify administrative bottlenecks. Who did what can be accountable while sensitive whereabouts and operational details are obscured. Whether publicly funded work requires a particular transparency policy or a standard civic encryption/access profile remains to be decided.
