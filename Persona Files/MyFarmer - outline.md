# MVP

"FarmConnect" connects farmers and customers. It emphasizes the personal relationship by facilitating produce discovery and aiding pickup and payment at the marketplace. A farmer can use a professional persona without exposing their underlying personal chain, which their Persona Manager maintains. After the exchange, both parties independently mark completion, choose whether their own optional comments are plaintext or encrypted, review each other's choices, and independently select Ready to commit.

### FarmConnect

#### Tagline:
"Your Local Harvest Connection"

#### Key Features:
- **Easy Uploads:** Farmers can quickly upload photos and details of their fresh produce.
- **Real-Time Notifications:** Customers receive instant alerts when new produce is available.
- **Simple Claims:** Customers choose a candidate offer and authorize a quantity under the Need’s terms; the farmer accepts before that commitment is established in the WORKING state.
- **Face-to-Face Interactions:** Maintain the personal touch of market day while optimizing efficiency.

#### Benefits:
- **For Farmers:**
  - Reduce waste by bringing only the produce that’s already claimed.
  - Build stronger relationships with customers through reliable availability.
  - Spend more time focusing on farming rather than guessing market demand.

- **For Customers:**
  - Know what fresh produce is available before heading to the market.
  - Ensure you get the freshest fruits and vegetables from your preferred farmers.
  - Enjoy the convenience of reserving produce while retaining the market experience.

### From order to handoff

The farmer uses a standard Need smart-contract template with custom quantity, departure, collection, and expiry terms. The WORKING state connects the committed Has and Needs while those terms execute. A driver scans and accepts the produce parcels; the farmer records the handoff. The truck leaves at its agreed time with the parcels actually aboard.

A Community pool may authorize one representative to receive the whole order. Individual customers collect their own parcels or expressly delegate that permission. An agreed expiry or no-deal outcome releases applicable objects under the contract and is recorded on-chain; personal filters may consider those outcomes when finding easy, reliable exchanges.

The complete communications and handoff record becomes identical receipts written simultaneously to required participants' chains and mutually verified. A farmer's facet can disclose produce quality and punctuality while names, addresses, and prices stay encrypted. Matching encrypted entries remain corroborable. A separate permissioned personal data stream can offer field observations through the same composable API. See [contract mechanics](../WORKING_CONTRACTS_AND_DELEGATION.md).
