# White paper — comparative novelty statement

**Status:** Approved framing for inclusion in the forthcoming Has-Needs white paper. This statement reports the current search outcome, not a proof of worldwide uniqueness or a patentability opinion.

## The architectural distinction

Has-Needs begins with the sovereign individual situated in their own lived environment. From that origin, it enables the discovery of nearby and progressively more distant possibilities for human cooperation. Its purpose is not to move human relationships into a platform, but to reduce friction in ordinary person-to-person interactions while preserving each participant's authority over identity, information, consent, commitments, and evidence.

The same Has/Need/WORKING grammar supports ordinary exchanges, community activity, emergencies, and recovery. This continuity is intentional: a person does not lose their familiar means of expressing needs, contributing value, choosing assistance, and preserving their own history merely because their circumstances become traumatic. Geographic relevance means a sphere of practical possibility, not a requirement to disclose someone's precise location; local-first does not mean local-only.

## Comparative finding

> **Even with the assistance of advanced publicly available AI tools, our investigation has yet to identify another program, project, or conceptual sociological framework comparable to Has-Needs in its comprehensive integration of individual sovereignty, geographically grounded human interaction, reciprocal value exchange, and continuity across ordinary life, crisis, and recovery.**

This is a bounded report of what the investigation has identified, not a claim that all prior art has been examined. Individual precedents exist for agent-centric computing, personal data control, local communication, requests-and-offers coordination, and economic-event modeling. The claimed distinction concerns their **integration and locus of authority**: the individual's environment and choices remain primary throughout discovery, consent, reciprocal commitment, and participant-held evidence.

The comparison must be tested against close architectural precedents—including Holochain, hREA/ValueFlows, Solid, and community requests-and-offers systems—using published specifications and implementation evidence. Novelty of the integrated design does not by itself establish patent novelty, deployed security properties, clinical trauma outcomes, or legal recognition of data rights.

## Editorial placement

Place this comparative finding after the explanation of the person-centered architecture and the ordinary-life-to-crisis-to-recovery continuum, before the technical comparison with prior systems. Do not substitute a blanket “world first” assertion or describe the architecture as simply a bundle of features.

## Execution and evidence

A Need is a smart contract with standard templates and custom options. The WORKING state connects its involved Has and Needs during execution, including delegated responsibility and controlled API streams. Outcomes—including agreed no-deal or expiry—produce identical receipts written simultaneously and mutually verified, with encrypted contents still corroborable. Community storage preserves permissions; organizational fulfillment informs the self-pruning tree. [Detailed mechanics and examples](./WORKING_CONTRACTS_AND_DELEGATION.md) also distinguish civic accountability from the still-open public-funding disclosure policy.
